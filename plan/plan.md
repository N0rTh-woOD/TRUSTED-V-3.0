# TRUSTED-V Bosch SDS–Inspired Public Website Redesign

TRUSTED-V will receive a cohesive enterprise editorial redesign across its entire signed-in informational website. The redesign will preserve the current TRUSTED-V identity, approved content, products, claims, availability labels, and conversion paths.

The look and interaction language will take close inspiration from the Bosch SDS reference: light, spacious, image-led, confident, structured, and technically credible—without reproducing Bosch logos, branded artwork, copy, or proprietary visual assets.

## Who it’s for

- Embedded engineers and technical decision-makers evaluating the RISC-V platform.
- Hardware, software, and ecosystem partners looking for clear product, service, and collaboration information.
- Internal TRUSTED-V team members reviewing the full informational website behind the existing access gate.

## Core features and experience

- A unified global shell with a compact, high-clarity header, TRUSTED-V logo at the top left, an accessible grouped product menu, a direct contact action, a responsive mobile drawer, and a structured footer.
- A more editorial, outcome-led homepage that retains the current TRUSTED-V modules and content while presenting them through confident hero composition, image-led feature stories, consistent card systems, proof-oriented bands, controlled carousels, and a decisive closing contact path.
- A repeatable visual template for all informational pages: clear page introduction, concise narrative blocks, meaningful visuals or technical evidence, related content, and a single relevant next action.
- A unified card, carousel, accordion, form, and CTA language with stable image ratios, readable hierarchy, consistent labels, keyboard support, touch support, and visible focus states.
- Redesigned product, platform, marketplace, services, partner, about, developer, resource, board-support, and contact experiences using the same system without changing their current public content or purpose.
- Existing public form fields, privacy/consent language, validations, data handling, and success flows retained while their presentation is brought into the new system.

## User flow

1. A signed-in visitor arrives on a statement-led homepage and quickly understands TRUSTED-V’s RISC-V platform position.
2. The visitor explores Products, Marketplace, Platform, Services, and About through concise navigation and grouped menus.
3. Each informational page leads from context to technical detail, supporting evidence, related destinations, and one clear next step.
4. A visitor who needs help, product access, partnership, or board support reaches the relevant existing form through a consistent contact path.
5. Forms keep the existing submission behavior and present the existing confirmation state in the refreshed visual system.

## UI/UX feel

- Light, open canvas with generous whitespace, strong dark typography, restrained TRUSTED-V blue and cyan accents, soft neutral surfaces, and thin structural dividers.
- Clean, modern sans-serif typography with stronger display headings, compact utility/navigation text, readable technical body copy, and a consistent type scale across desktop and mobile.
- Large, authentic, meaning-bearing TRUSTED-V imagery and diagrams used deliberately in heroes, feature stories, and cards; no Bosch SDS imagery or brand marks.
- Editorial section rhythm: positioning, supporting narrative, visual proof, grouped cards or modules, then one next-step action.
- Meaningful motion only: short transitions for navigation, cards, accordions, and carousel changes; no automatic carousel rotation; keyboard, touch, and reduced-motion behavior remain supported.
- Desktop uses compact navigation and grouped mega-menu panels; mobile uses a clear drawer with accordion groups and persistent contact access.

## Implementation phases

### Phase 1 — Complete informational-site visual redesign (built now)
- Establish the shared TRUSTED-V visual system: type scale, colors, spacing, surfaces, dividers, motion, buttons, cards, forms, carousels, and responsive behavior.
- Redesign the global header, accessible product mega-menu, mobile navigation, and footer.
- Redesign the homepage and every current signed-in informational page with the new system while preserving their current content, page purpose, public routes, technical claims, availability labels, and form behavior.
- Refresh all public form and confirmation-state presentation without changing fields, validations, privacy language, or submission behavior.
- Verify desktop and mobile usability, keyboard navigation, visual continuity, form flows, and motion behavior.

### Phase 2 — Content-proof and resource expansion (future)
- Add only approved, evidence-backed case studies, technical resources, recognition, or partner proof modules where source material becomes available.
- Introduce resource or story browsing patterns only if real content and ownership are confirmed.

### Phase 3 — Design governance and launch readiness (future)
- Create a concise visual and editorial governance guide for future pages and campaigns.
- Complete public-launch review for accessibility, performance, legal/privacy links, content ownership, and approval of any new proof points or imagery.

## Assumptions

- Phase 1 covers the entire existing signed-in informational website, not only the homepage.
- The redesign closely follows the Bosch SDS site’s visual system and interaction posture, but remains recognizably TRUSTED-V.
- TRUSTED-V’s current logo remains at the top left of the navigation and keeps its current hero placement/treatment.
- Existing approved public copy, product names, partner names, technical specifications, availability labels, claims, routes, and form behavior remain unchanged.
- Existing TRUSTED-V assets are reused where suitable; any new imagery must be approved, original, or properly licensed. Bosch SDS logos, wordmarks, visual assets, copy, and proprietary brand elements will not be used.
- Current authentication gating, account behavior, administration retirement, and backend capabilities are outside this visual redesign.
- Search, language/region selection, new legal pages, new social links, and new content categories are not added unless separately requested and supplied with approved content.
